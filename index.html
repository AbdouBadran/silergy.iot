🚀 Our Mission
Driven by Real Impact, Not Just Grades

When we set out to build our project, we didn't want to create something routine just to pass. Instead, we focused on solving a real-world problem through smart technology.

🐛 The Challenge & Solution
Smart Environment for Silk Production

Our country produces silk, which relies entirely on silkworms. These silkworms require very specific conditions to thrive. Our solution is an automated system that maintains the ideal environment for optimal silk production.

📡 Sensors & Climate Control
Precision Monitoring

We equipped the system with smart sensors to continuously track key environmental factors:

  • 🌡️ Temperature
  • 💧 Humidity
  • 🍃 Air Purity
☀️ Power & Sustainability
Eco-Friendly & Continuous Energy

To ensure 24/7 uninterrupted operation, the system is powered by renewable energy:

  • ☀️ Solar Energy with MPPT: Uses Maximum Power Point Tracking technology to harvest solar energy with peak efficiency.
  • 🔋 Battery Backup: Guarantees continuous system operation during the night or overcast weather.
